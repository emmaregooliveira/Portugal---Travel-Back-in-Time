Portugal is one of the world’s oldest nation-states, with over 800 years of history and stable territorial borders. Get to know more about the country to learn of the lives on this side of the Atlantic.

Growing up in Canada, I had the privilege to learn both the history and language of Portugal from my immigrant parents and a Portuguese school, which they enrolled me in. I desire to give the Portuguese children of immigrants an opportunity to learn about where they are from and what their families went through to get them here.

THE HISTORY OF OUR HOME

Vou voltar a casa.
I'm going back home.

Between Lisbon and New York lies the 9 Azores islands and Madeira, which is not considered apart of the Azores. These islands are a part of Portugal and are located 1'370 kilometers west of what the Portuguese call the “mainland”. The islands are where most emigrants in Canada and America are from. Corvo, Faial, Flores, Graciosa, Pico, Santa Maria, São Jorge, São Miguel and Terceira.
